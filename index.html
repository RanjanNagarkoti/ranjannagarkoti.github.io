about

Building integrations that keep working after launch

I work on Shopify integrations where two platforms need to stay in sync — Square POS, and before that, Walmart Marketplace. I'm the solo lead full-stack developer on DPL Square: I traced a recurring inventory bug to race conditions in the sync pipeline and rebuilt it on AWS SQS and Lambda, and no new inventory-sync issues have been reported since. On the Walmart side, I rebuilt the category mapping from 70 categories to 6,942 and built two-way product sync so updates no longer needed manual checking.

I also work on the Shopify admin side of the app itself — I brought its load time (LCP) down from 2.8s to under 1.5s, which helped it earn Shopify's Built for Shopify status, and added skeleton loading states across the product. I'm finishing a B.Sc. (Hons) in Software Engineering at UCSI University alongside this, and picked up 1st runner-up at the SkillLab x SecurityPal hackathon in 2023.
